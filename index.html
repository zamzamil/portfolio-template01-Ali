Information

Studio
Seoyeon is interested in the small yet meaningful moments found within everyday life. By observing posture, repetitive behaviours, interactions with objects, and traces left within a space, she explores how ordinary situations shape emotional experience. For her, design extends beyond functional problem-solving; it can offer a way to recognise and communicate emotions that are difficult to articulate. Her personal and cultural experiences provide an important lens through which she interprets these moments.

Closit

2026 RCA GRADSHOW
Furniture
Solo Project
Cherry wood, Aluminium
Ritual Design
2026
1400 × 1000 × 1000 mm
Closit explores how everyday rituals can shape emotional wellbeing. By transforming the repetitive act of folding clothes into a deliberate interaction, the project reframes a familiar domestic habit as a moment of reflection rather than a task to be completed. Instead of concealing the accumulation of clothing, the furniture makes it visible, revealing how ordinary objects can quietly record our routines, emotions, and patterns of living. Closit proposes furniture not simply as functional equipment, but as a medium that encourages more mindful relationships with ourselves and our everyday environment.

Low Tide

Publication
Isidro Marsh
Photography / English
2019
ISBN: 979-8-6116-0038
246 × 292 mm
168 pp. Softcover
A photographic survey of a single estuary shot over four years. The layout follows the rhythm of the tide itself — full-bleed spreads at high water, tightly cropped single images at low water — with captions relegated to an index at the back so nothing interrupts the sequence.

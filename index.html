Studio
Seoyeon is interested in the small yet meaningful moments found within everyday life. By observing posture, repetitive behaviours, interactions with objects, and traces left within a space, she explores how ordinary situations shape emotional experience. For her, design extends beyond functional problem-solving; it can offer a way to recognise and communicate emotions that are difficult to articulate. Her personal and cultural experiences provide an important lens through which she interprets these moments.

A photographic survey of a single estuary shot over four years. The layout follows the rhythm of the tide itself — full-bleed spreads at high water, tightly cropped single images at low water — with captions relegated to an index at the back so nothing interrupts the sequence.
